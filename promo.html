Meet Mishmishتعرّفوا على مشمش

Every story has the same small apricot cat in it. He sits by the gate, waits at the classroom window, turns up at break, refuses to move from the shade on Sports Day, and even sneaks into the National Day photograph. He never talks — he is simply always there.
